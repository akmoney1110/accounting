The Drying Rack (How the Sun Removes Water)

See how fresh cashew from farmers becomes dry cashew ready for the market

How it works: When farmers bring fresh cashew, it contains water. You spread them in the sun for 2-3 days. The water disappears (evaporates), and the cashew becomes lighter. You can sell the dry ones, but you cannot sell the water that was removed.
