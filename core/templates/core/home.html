What We Do

We prepare dry cashew nuts for the market.

Our role is focused on sourcing and preparing Raw Cashew Nuts for trade. We work with agricultural communities, consolidate harvested nuts and carefully dry them before storage and sale.

We do not process the nuts into kernels. Our product is dry Raw Cashew Nut (RCN) supplied to buyers and markets that require quality dried cashew for further processing or trade.

Direct Agricultural Sourcing

We work within farming and aggregation networks to source harvested raw cashew nuts.

Controlled Sun-Drying

Nuts are spread and regularly turned across suitable drying surfaces to encourage consistent moisture reduction.

Quality-Focused Handling

We pay close attention to cleanliness, moisture condition, storage and general quality throughout the handling process.

Our Supply Journey

From Farm to Export

A straightforward agricultural supply chain built around careful sourcing, drying, storage and reliable delivery.

01

Sourcing

Farm Procurement

We source raw cashew nuts through agricultural communities, farmers and aggregation networks, bringing harvested nuts into our supply chain for careful handling and drying.

02

Intake

Inspection & Consolidation

Incoming nuts are inspected and consolidated by batch. We pay attention to visible quality, foreign matter, general condition and other characteristics important to the intended market.

03

Drying

Natural Sun-Drying

Solar Drying

Freshly harvested nuts are spread across suitable drying yards and regularly turned to encourage even exposure to sunlight and gradual moisture reduction.

04

Quality

Moisture & Quality Checks

Drying progress is monitored so that the nuts reach the condition required for appropriate storage, handling and sale.

05

Storage

Dry RCN Storage

Once adequately dried, the nuts are consolidated and stored under suitable conditions while awaiting buyer requirements, transportation or export.

06

Final Stage

Supply & Export

Our dry Raw Cashew Nuts are supplied to buyers and prepared for transportation and export according to agreed commercial and logistical requirements.

Our Warehouse

Built around careful handling.

Our warehouse serves as an important link between agricultural sourcing and final delivery. After drying and quality checks, cashews are consolidated and held under suitable storage conditions while orders and transportation arrangements are coordinated.

Good warehouse management helps us maintain batch organization, reduce unnecessary handling and prepare commercial quantities for loading and transportation.

Organized Batch Storage

Storage Monitoring

03

Once the shipment is ready, appropriate transport is arranged to move the cargo from the warehouse or collection point to the designated port or export terminal.

04

Port Operations

Cargo Handling & Loading

Cargo is delivered to the designated export terminal where it proceeds through applicable cargo handling, inspection and loading procedures before departure.

05

Ocean Freight

International Sea Shipment

06

Who We Are

From local enterprise
to a broader vision.

Dikubs is a diversified enterprise based in Anyigba, Kogi State, Nigeria, with business interests spanning agricultural commodities, retail and bakery operations.

Our business is built around serving people and businesses with products they can rely on. From the sourcing and international trade of quality dried cashew nuts to everyday supermarket shopping and freshly baked products, we continue to develop businesses that respond to real market needs.

We believe that a successful business should do more than generate revenue. It should create opportunities, build relationships, support its community and continuously improve the value it provides to customers and partners.

As Dikubs grows, our ambition is to build a respected Nigerian business group with strong operational standards, trusted relationships and the capacity to serve markets both within Nigeria and internationally.
